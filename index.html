How it works

From the first call to a live app

You are not handed a zip file and left alone. We stay with the project through every stage, and you see progress on your own phone as we go.

  1. Discovery call

    We talk through the idea, who it is for and how it makes money. You get a clear scope, timeline and price before anything starts.

    written plan

  2. Design

    Screens, game art, icons and the flow of the app, shaped with you until it looks and feels right.

    mockups & art

  3. Build

    We write the app and send you regular test builds to install and try. Your feedback goes straight into the next build.

    working builds

  4. Testing

    Real testers, real phones. We run Google's closed testing for you and fix what they find.

    stable release

  5. Launch

    Store listing, graphics, privacy policy, Data safety, and the production release on Google Play.

    live listing

  6. After launch

    Updates, new features, ad tuning, reviews and promo videos. We stay on as your tech partner.

    ongoing support

Google Play services

Already have an app? We'll get it on the store.

Google Play has a lot of rules, and a rejected release can cost weeks. We have taken our own games through every step, so we can take yours through too, as a standalone service or as part of a build.

New personal developer accounts must run a closed test with at least 12 testers for 14 days before they can apply for production. We plan and manage that whole process.
